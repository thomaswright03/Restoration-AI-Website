Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Who we are

Room Designer 3D is run by Wright AI Solutions, LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when businesses use Room Designer 3D and when homeowners use a business's designer.

Businesses that sign up

We keep your account email and your business details (name, web address, phone, email, legal name and labor prices). Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and we never see it. Payments are handled by Stripe: we receive your plan and payment status, not your full card number.

Projects you save

When you save a design as a project, we store what you enter about the job: your client's name, phone and email, the job address, the type of job, its status, start date and notes, together with the design and its estimate. That is your clients' personal information, collected by you under your own privacy promises to them, and you're responsible for having their permission to keep it with us. It's stored in our database (Supabase), only you can see it, signed in to your account, and it's deleted when you delete the project or your account.

Homeowners using a designer

Your 3D design stays in your browser, where it's saved so you can pick up where you left off. A link to your design carries the design itself; we don't store it. Only when you press Send Request do we receive your name, phone, email and project details (which include your design), which we store for the business whose designer you used. That business decides how to use them and you should contact it about your request. A business can also save a design as a project in its own account and enter your name, contact details and address there; that is its record of your job, and you should ask that business about it.

Who helps us run the service

Vercel hosts the website, Supabase stores accounts and requests, Stripe handles payments, and an email provider may send new requests to the business. They only process data to provide those services to us. We don't sell personal information.

How long we keep it

Account and business details are kept while the account is open and deleted after it is closed, except billing records we must keep by law. Homeowner requests are kept until the business deletes them or closes its account. Saved projects, with the client details in them, are kept until the business deletes the project or closes its account.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Businesses can edit their details, delete projects and requests, and delete their whole account (business, projects, requests, subscription and sign-in) from their account page; deleting is immediate and can't be undone. Homeowners can ask the business they contacted, or us, to delete a request. Sign-in and your language and theme choices use your browser's storage; we don't use advertising cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell account holders by email.
