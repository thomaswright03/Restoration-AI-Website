What you get

Everything a bathroom lead needs

A measured 3D room and floor plan

Enter the room's size in feet and inches, then drag fixtures in 3D or on the floor plan. Clearances are checked as you go, and one tap arranges the room.

Real product models

Toilets, tubs, showers, vanities, sinks and mirrors are 3D models of real manufacturer products, with real tile, flooring and paint finishes.

Estimates at your prices

A rough, non-binding labor and materials estimate using the labor rates you set, with a PDF you can give your customer.

Saved projects

Save each design as a project with your client's name, contact details and address, and reopen it any time from My projects.

English, Spanish and Portuguese
